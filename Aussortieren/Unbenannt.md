The mythological figure that can best represent n8n is

**Hermes**. 

Hermes, the Greek god (known as Mercury in Roman mythology), is an excellent representation for a workflow automation and integration platform like n8n for several key reasons: 

- **Messenger of the Gods:** Hermes' primary role is to act as the emissary and herald of the Olympians, carrying messages between the divine, mortal, and underworld realms. This directly parallels how n8n acts as a messenger, enabling the flow of data and communication between different applications and services.
- **Speed and Efficiency:** He is known for his speed, often depicted with winged sandals.
- **God of Trade and Commerce:** Hermes is also the patron of trade and commerce.
- **Cunning and Ingenuity:** Described as cunning, clever, and a trickster, Hermes often uses his shrewdness for the benefit of humans.
- **Guide and Conductor:** He is a guide, or _psychopomp_, who conducts souls to the underworld and assists heroes on their quests.
